Field notes

World map + time dial

The “Google Earth of history” answer to “I see 1776 and can’t imagine the world.” Drag the index along the bottom rail and every border on the map moves with it.

Territories are real research data — 18 snapshots between 3000 BCE and 1994 — coloured by sovereign. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, and click any spot to drill a core sample through everything that ever happened there.

Border data: aourednik/historical-basemaps (GPL-3.0). Scholarly approximation — historical borders are fuzzy and contested by nature, so read every edge as an argument rather than a fact.

Survey — the system in one screen

Chrome for a full-viewport historical atlas. The ground is chart film in light and bathymetric blue-black in dark. The chrome has exactly one accent hue — minium — and it means one thing: where you are. Every other state is carried by the neutral ramp or by form. The eight data hues below are a separate system, drawn on the canvas, never in the chrome. Use the moon button in the rail to check both themes.
